Responsible Modeling & Transparency

Prediction Uncertainties

PM2.5 forecasts are estimates with ±8–12 µg/m³ error. Accuracy drops during Diwali, stubble burning, or sudden weather shifts. Not a substitute for regulatory monitoring.

Scenario Outputs

Scenario results are modeled estimates using traffic indicators, not direct observations. They show directional change, not guaranteed outcomes.

Traffic Data

Traffic-volume information is used as a contextual proxy. We do not claim real-time vehicle counts or exact emission inventories without loop-detector validation.

Historical Validation

Model validated on 20% hold-out (n=4,892 hours). Metrics load from model_results.json. Re-train monthly with new observations.
